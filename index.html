Shelf

Four columns per record, in sheet order: Cover URL, Description, First released, Pressing year — tab-separated. Tap in, select all, copy, then paste into cell F2 of the Collection tab and Sheets fills all four columns down. Once a cell has a value the app uses it and never looks that record up again, on any device. Add a record and it resolves on its own, then shows up here for the next paste. To redo one record, clear its cells in the sheet and the app resolves it afresh.
